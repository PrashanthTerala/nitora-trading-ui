## 4. Proposed visual style

Use the site's dark theme tokens, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, with one idea per scene.

- **Charts:** the site's own SVG, drawn on progressively with clip-path reveals rather than slideshow cuts. The prototype does this already.
- **Captions:** a short English on-screen label per scene. The user's choice for te was to keep on-screen text in English; burned-in subtitles per language are a separate, optional track.
- **Numbers:** any figure on screen is computed by the engine at render time. It is never typed by hand.
- **Risk:** each video carries the lesson's warning callout as its own scene, plus a fixed closing card: "educational, synthetic data, not financial advice".
- **Restraint:** no stock footage, no AI-generated imagery, no presenter avatar.

Prototype frames have been inspected (engulfing, 131 s, en). The definition, pattern and trap
scenes read well. The style still needs your sign-off on the pilot.

## 7. Architecture proposal (adapting `video/`)

```
video/
  manifests/curriculum.json          inventory (exists)
  lessons/<module>/<lesson>/
    script.en.json                   scenes: id, narration, on-screen text, visual cue, source line refs
    script.te.json  script.hi.json  script.es.json
    review.json                      per-language reviewer, date, sha256 of the reviewed script
  scripts/
    inventory.mjs                    exists
    narrate.(py|ts)                  -> TtsProvider adapter; writes narration/<lesson>/<lang>/*.wav + durations
    check.mjs                        source-hash drift, term list, number check, length budget
  src/
    lessons/<Lesson>.tsx             scenes shared across languages
    timing.ts                        per-language scene lengths from that language's narration
  out/<lesson>.<lang>.mp4            one file per language (not committed)
```

Key changes from the prototype:

1. **Per-language timing.** Each language gets its own scene lengths, worked out from its own audio, and its own MP4. The prototype's shared max-length timeline left about 30 s of dead air in the English version. Visual beats are written as fractions of each scene, so they stretch with the narration.
2. **Traceability.** Every scene names the MDX lines it comes from. `check.mjs` fails when the lesson's sha256 has changed since the script was approved.
3. **No hand-typed numbers.** A number spoken in narration must match a value the engine computes for that figure, and the check enforces it.
4. **Review gate.** A video cannot be rendered as "final" until `review.json` holds a sign-off for that language and script hash. Publishing to the site is a separate, explicitly authorised step.

## 9. Pilot plan: `m05-indicators/03-ma-crossovers`

This is the closest verified lesson to the directive's "50 EMA vs 200 EMA" preference (sha256 `5c5b61c2…207f`, 1,257 prose words, 6 takeaways, 5 quiz questions, a math callout and a whipsaw danger callout).

**Content issues to resolve before scripting.** The video must not repeat these.

1. **SMA vs EMA.** The lesson defines the golden and death crosses on the **50/200 SMA**. Its figure plots **9/21 EMA**. The directive asks for 50/200 EMA. Choose one:
   - (a) teach 50/200 **SMA**, as the lesson does, and mention EMA as a variant;
   - (b) change the lesson to EMA first.

   Recommended: (a). Changing the video's facts away from the site's own lesson is the wrong order.
2. **Frequency contradiction.** The lesson's table says the 50/200 cross happens "roughly one or two" times a year on daily charts. Its text says "a handful of times per decade". These cannot both stand.
   - The engine can count the crossings on long synthetic series, but synthetic data cannot settle a claim about real markets.
   - Recommended: soften both statements to "rarely, a few times over many years", or source them.
3. **Unsourced claims.** "Win rate sometimes under 40%" and "tested more than any other idea" have no source. Either cite them or remove them from the narration.
4. **Missing prerequisite.** The lesson depends on `02-moving-averages`, but it declares no prerequisites.

**Pilot deliverables, after your approval:**
- the English script, traced to its source lines;
- a 50/200 figure computed by the engine;
- an en render;
- te, hi and es scripts with the term lists;
- per-language renders with their own timing;
- a QA sheet for each language;
- a stop for review.

Visuals will be computed with the engine's `sma`/`ema` (both tested), and nothing else.
